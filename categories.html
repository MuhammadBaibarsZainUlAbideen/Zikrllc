Product Categories

Product Division

Zikr LLC has multiple product divisions, selling products and services through GSA schedules, DOD contracts, and various state, local, and federal government bidding portals. Our goal is to serve our customers with the best possible solutions through a combination of technology expertise and strategic partnerships.

Our network of partners enables us to assemble a large team of specialists with deep knowledge and unmatched passion for delivering high-quality products, technology services, and presale support. We cater to federal, state, and local governments as well as the commercial sector.

IT Products

We deal in all types of I.T hardware and software products, offering consultancy and bulk discounts for large orders. Our product range includes:

  • Power Supplies & Protection

Non-I.T Division
